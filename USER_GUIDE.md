# Tandem user guide

Tandem helps two people prepare for useful 1-on-1s, keep shared commitments visible, and build a record of growth over time.

## Try the demo first

Open the [public demo](https://lead-together-now.lovable.app/demo) to explore a sample coaching check-in without creating an account. You can change the agenda, write sample notes, complete actions, and reveal a sample AI summary. Nothing in the demo is saved.

## 1. Create an account and workspace

1. Choose **Get started** and sign up with email and password, or use Google if it is available on your installation.
2. Complete email confirmation when your installation requires it.
3. Enter a workspace name. A workspace can represent an organization, team, program, cohort, or any other group.

The first person to create a workspace is its owner. New workspaces receive all nine starter coaching templates automatically.

## 2. Invite people

Workspace owners and admins can invite people from **Settings**.

1. Enter the person's email address.
2. Choose **Member** or **Admin**.
3. Select **Invite**.
4. Next to the pending invitation, select **Copy link** and send that link to the person.

The invitation link opens the sign-up form with their email address already filled in. They join the workspace automatically the first time they sign in with that exact address.

## 2a. Scheduling 1-on-1s in your calendar

Tandem keeps the rhythm of each 1-on-1 and marks a relationship **due** when it has been longer than the chosen gap. To put a session in your own calendar, select **Schedule** on the **Your 1-on-1s** or **People** page, or **Add to calendar** inside a session.

1. Pick the date, time, and length.
2. Leave **Repeat on the usual rhythm** ticked for a recurring series.
3. Choose **Google**, **Outlook**, or **Apple / other** (a calendar file that works on iPhone, iPad, Mac, Windows, and Android).

The event is added to your calendar only. Invite the other person from your calendar so they receive it too.

### Roles

- **Owner:** created the workspace and can manage it.
- **Admin:** can manage members, invitations, relationships, templates, AI, and branding.
- **Member:** can take part in 1-on-1s and create relationships they lead.

Admin access does not provide access to other people's meeting notes.

## 3. Set up who meets with whom

Open **People**, then choose the two participants and their relationship:

- **Reports to:** a leader and another participant; also supports skip-level visibility for shared content.
- **Coaching / mentoring partner:** a peer-style coaching relationship.

For each relationship, choose:

- A published coaching template
- Weekly, every two weeks, monthly, or quarterly cadence

Owners and admins can configure relationships for anyone. A leader can also create and manage relationships they lead. The **Hand off** option transfers a 1-on-1 and its history to a new leader. Removing a relationship also removes its meetings, so use that option carefully.

## 4. Start and run a 1-on-1

The **Your 1-on-1s** page shows each of your relationships, when you last met, and whether the next meeting is due.

1. Select **Start 1-on-1**.
2. Add or complete agenda topics.
3. Use the leader and participant questions to guide the conversation.
4. Write shared notes; each person can also keep their own private notes.
5. Create action items, assign an owner, and add a due date when useful.
6. Mark actions complete from the meeting or the home page.

Open actions remain visible until completed. The latest published template is copied into the new meeting; changing a template later does not change any meeting already created.

## Notes and privacy

- **Your shared notes** are visible to the other participant and to anyone explicitly allowed to read shared content, such as an applicable skip-level leader.
- **The other participant's shared notes** remain attributed to that person.
- **Private notes** are available to both people and visible only to their author.
- **Private notes are never sent to AI.**

Workspace owners and admins do not receive special access to meeting notes. Permissions are enforced in the database, not only by hiding controls on the page.

## Action items

Action items belong to a relationship and can have an owner and due date. Unfinished actions appear on the home page and carry forward until they are completed. Either participant can use them to keep commitments visible between conversations.

## Growth timeline and export

Choose **Timeline** beside a relationship to review its meeting history. The timeline brings past conversations together in date order and can be exported as Markdown for a portable text record.

Only export or share a timeline when doing so is appropriate for the people involved and your organization's policies.

## Coaching templates

Open **Templates** to view the question library. Each template contains separate prompts for the leader and the other participant.

The nine starter templates are:

- Weekly check-in
- Coaching check-in
- Career growth
- Quarterly goals review
- First 90 days
- Skip-level
- Project debrief
- Back from time away
- Getting back on track

Admins can create a blank template, copy an existing template, edit questions one per line, and control whether a template is published. Only published templates are available for new meetings.

## Optional AI

When AI is configured by the host and enabled in **Settings**, Tandem can draft meeting summaries and suggest action items. AI receives shared meeting content only. It never receives private notes.

If AI is unavailable or disabled, agendas, notes, actions, templates, timelines, and exports continue to work normally.

## Brand a workspace

Owners and admins can open **Settings → Branding** and set:

- Product name
- Square logo image link
- Accent color
- Welcome headline and message
- Help email address or web link

The logo field accepts a publicly reachable image URL; direct file upload is not currently included. See the [branding guide](./BRANDING.md) for details and deeper rebranding options.

## Use multiple workspaces

Choose **Create another workspace** in **Settings** to keep separate groups or programs distinct. Use the workspace switcher in the Tandem header to move between them.

## Install Tandem on a phone or tablet

Tandem can be added to a home screen and opened like an app.

### iPhone and iPad

1. Open the Tandem address in Safari.
2. Tap **Share**.
3. Choose **Add to Home Screen**.
4. Confirm the name and select **Add**.

### Android

1. Open the Tandem address in Chrome.
2. Open the browser menu.
3. Choose **Install app** or **Add to Home screen**.
4. Confirm the installation.

An internet connection is required. Tandem does not currently store meeting notes for offline access.

## Get help

If a workspace has a help contact, it appears in the footer. For the open-source project, use the [GitHub repository](https://github.com/mckinneylibrary/leadershiptandem) to review the source or open an issue.
